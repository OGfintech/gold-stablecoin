## Implications for AUSRX

### Validation Points

1. **Market Size:** $55B+ trade finance market validates opportunity
2. **LC Dominance:** 24%+ market share confirms LC importance
3. **Digital Growth:** Blockchain adoption trend supports Smart LC model
4. **Bank Interest:** HSBC, JPMorgan investing in tokenization
5. **Pricing:** 1-2% LC fees are industry standard

### Competitive Advantages

1. **Gold-backed collateral** - More trusted than fiat
2. **24/7 operation** - Unlike bank hours
3. **Lower fees** - No bank intermediary markup
4. **Transparency** - Blockchain verification
5. **Speed** - Minutes vs days

2. Regulatory uncertainty
