Global trade finance is broken. The $18 trillion annual trade finance industry still relies on:

### Smart Letter of Credit: 3-Stage Payment Release

1. **Stage 1 (30%)** — Released when goods ship, verified by Bill of Lading hash
2. **Stage 2 (50%)** — Released upon customs clearance, verified by import documentation
3. **Stage 3 (20%)** — Released on delivery confirmation and quality inspection
