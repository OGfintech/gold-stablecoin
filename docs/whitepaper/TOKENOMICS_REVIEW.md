## Executive Summary

**Key Decision Required:** Should AUSRX include traditional crypto allocation mechanisms (pre-mine, team tokens, investor tokens, airdrops)?

**Recommendation:** No token allocations. Use equity for team/investors, fee incentives for users. Every token must be backed by user-deposited gold.

## 2. The Core Difference: Gold-Backed vs Typical Crypto

| | Typical Crypto | Gold-Backed (AUSRX) |
|---|----------------|----------------------|
| **Token Value** | Speculative | Physical gold |
| **Giving Away Tokens** | $0 cost | Giving away gold |
| **Creating New Tokens** | Free (just code) | Requires buying gold |
| **Backing Requirement** | None | 100% gold reserves |

**Implication:** Traditional crypto allocation mechanisms don't translate directly. Every token given away = real gold given away.

## 3. Allocation Mechanisms Analysis

### 3.1 Pre-Mine

| Decision | Reasoning |
|----------|-----------|
| ❌ **NO** | Every token needs gold backing. Pre-mining = buying gold with company funds. Defeats "user deposit = user's gold" narrative. |

---

### 3.2 Team Allocation

**Arguments FOR:**
- Team should have "skin in the game"
- Aligns incentives with token success
- Standard for attracting talent

**Arguments AGAINST:**
- Every token needs gold = company must BUY gold for team
- Could look like "insiders getting free gold"
- Undermines "100% user-backed" claim

| Decision | Alternative |
|----------|-------------|
| ❌ No Token Allocation | ✅ **Equity in company instead.** Team gets shares, not gold tokens. Or: profit-sharing from platform fees. |

---

### 3.3 Investor Tokens

**Arguments FOR:**
- Need capital to build platform
- Investors want token exposure

**Arguments AGAINST:**
- "Discounted tokens" = someone got gold cheaper
- Could trigger securities regulations
- Undermines fair pricing narrative

| Decision | Alternative |
|----------|-------------|
| ❌ No Discounted Tokens | ✅ **Raise via equity investment.** Convertible notes → equity. Investors can buy tokens at full price like everyone else. |

---

### 3.4 Airdrops

**Why NOT for Gold-Backed:**
- Airdrop = giving away real gold value
- Extremely expensive marketing
- Attracts mercenaries, not long-term users

| Instead of Airdrops | Use These Alternatives |
|---------------------|------------------------|
| Free tokens | **Fee rebates** (reduced fees for early users) |
| Free tokens | **Referral bonuses** (fee credits, not tokens) |
| Free tokens | **Boosted staking APY** (6% first 90 days) |
| Free tokens | **Zero mint fee** for first $1,000 deposit |

## 5. CRITICAL: Where Does 5% APY Come From?

⚠️ **This is the most important question for the whitepaper and regulatory compliance.**

### Possible Yield Sources

| Source | How It Works | Sustainability |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **Platform Fees** | Trading/transfer fees redistributed to stakers | ✅ Sustainable |
| **Gold Lending** | Institutions borrow gold, pay interest | ✅ Sustainable |
| **Treasury Yield** | Company invests reserves conservatively | ✅ Sustainable |
| **Company Subsidy** | Company pays from operating budget | ❌ Not sustainable |

**Action Required:** Define the yield source clearly before whitepaper publication. Regulators will ask this question.

## 6. Fee Structure (DECIDED)

**Strategy:** 10% below market rates, with flexibility to adjust.

| Action | Fee | Recipient | Competitor (PAXG) |
|--------|-----|-----------|-------------------|
| **Minting** | 0.027% | Platform | 0.03% + 1oz min |
| **Transfer** | 0.018% | Platform | 0.02% |
| **Redemption (Fiat)** | 0.18% | Platform + HSBC | 0.2%+ |
| **Redemption (Physical)** | 1.35% | Platform + HSBC + Logistics | N/A |
| **Marketplace Trade** | 0.225% | Platform | 0.25% (standard) |
| **Staking Entry/Exit** | 0% | - | N/A |

**Rationale:** Undercut competitors on price while providing superior value (HSBC custody, staking yield, certificate verification). Fees subject to adjustment based on market conditions.
