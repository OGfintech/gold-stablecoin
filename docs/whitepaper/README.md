## 🎯 Key Research Findings

### HSBC Custody Model
- Certificate-triggered minting (not crypto company vaults)
- Top-6 global gold broker supply chain
- Regulatory compliance advantages over PAXG/XAUT

### Tokenomics Decision
- **No pre-mine** - All tokens backed 1:1 by gold
- **No team allocation** - Use equity instead
- **No investor tokens** - Raise via equity
- **No airdrops** - Not needed for utility token

### Staking Yield (5% APY)
- Source: Smart Letter of Credit fees (1-2% per trade)
- Market: $55B trade finance, $13B+ LC segment
- Target: Commodity trades requiring payment guarantees

### Market Opportunity
- Global Trade: $32+ trillion
- China Trade: $6.54 trillion
- China-Latin America: $550 billion (+6.5% YoY)
- Mexico-China: $139.7 billion (+8.2% YoY)
